    Scenarios on your inputs

    Three-year roadmap with a data gate before Year 2 money

    Prove on coaters in Year 1, invest on a proven baseline in Year 2, scale to granulation trains in Year 3.

    Year 1 · prove at zero capex

    • Pull 12 months of order, stage and BOM data
    • BOM commonality study across HSM, FBE/FBC and SMARTCOATER sizes
    • Design-freeze gate with a plant-head sponsor
    • Early POs on pooled long-lead parts
    • Manual finite-capacity board
    • Customer-witnessed FAT pilot with 1-2 lead customers
    • Pilot family: SMARTCOATER

    Year 2 · invest on a proven baseline

    • Pre-stage kits for the top 2 sizes of coaters, HSM and FBE
    • Finite-capacity scheduling on the ERP
    • Family bays in the expansion shed
    • Validation engine on the coater line

    Year 3 · scale

    • Pre-staging extended to GT trains
    • Remote or hybrid FAT where customers accept it
    • 55-65% internal compression on catalog orders
    • Blended 30-45% at maturity

    Year 1 go/no-go gate

    Success factors

    • Cross-functional team across design, procurement, production, QA and controls
    • Plant-head sponsor who enforces the design-freeze gate
    • One pilot family before any scale-up
    • Customer QA engaged early on the FAT protocol

    Backup triggers watched by this tower

    • Kit older than 90 days: redeploy review
    • Kit value above ceiling: pause pre-stage builds
    • Change after freeze: priced delta and sponsor sign-off
    • Leveraged FAT refused: full SAT, impact re-forecast
